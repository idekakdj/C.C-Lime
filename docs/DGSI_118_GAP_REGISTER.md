Prepared October 1, 2026. Engineering mapping for the current personal student calendar. **No DGSI conformity finding.** PHIPA remains excluded by the owner; DGSI remains a requested requirement pending its healthcare-scope decision.

The [official standard](https://dgc-cgn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/FINAL_CAN_DGSI_118_R-2026_EN.pdf) is CAN/DGSI 118:2023 (R2026), first edition reaffirmed February 2026. Its scope concerns healthcare organizations. C.C. Lime has no clinical care, patient records, medical devices or virtual-care workflow. The proposed clinical exclusions below need an operator/qualified-assessor decision; they are not silently removed or marked compliant. Generic student-product preparations remain useful regardless of that decision.

## Method and completion rule

All 131 numbered leaf requirements in sections 4–10 have an explicit row. Lettered lists remain part of their parent requirement and must be reviewed in full against the source before closure. Sections 1–3 provide scope/references/definitions; informative annexes were distinguished from requirements. Rows link C.C. Lime's existing preparation and exact task boundary, without reproducing the standard's requirements or pretending a general task link is complete evidence. Some rows combine implemented safeguards and an organizational gap; none are marked closed.

The qualified reviewer must record applicability/reason, every parent subpart, named owner, approved control, dated operational/technical evidence, residual gap/treatment and next review. Add the referenced 104 mapping once legally obtained. Then validate the agreed scope through the requested conformance assessment. A proposed exclusion needs signed rationale. A draft runbook or client test cannot establish an operating organization-wide requirement.
