# Proposed privacy notice and rights procedure

October 1, 2026. Draft for the Ontario operator's legal/privacy review. **Not published or approved.** PHIPA is outside the owner-selected scope. Actual PIPEDA applicability depends on the operator and activity; no conclusion is inferred from a student calendar or an Ontario address. Use the [OPC business guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/) for the applicable review.

## Notice text ready for operator completion

Operator and privacy contact: **pending legal name, contact channel and address**. Effective version/date: **pending approval**. Do not publish with these fields missing or state that C.C. Lime is certified/compliant.

C.C. Lime uses your account identity and calendar information to provide sign-in, scheduling, reminders, personalization, progress history and synchronization. Your email, provider identity and optional profile name/photo are associated with your account. Events, tasks, courses, occurrence/completion history and appearance preferences are saved locally and, when you use a verified cloud account, synchronized with Firebase. You can instead use a separate local calendar. Google sign-in requests identity information through Google; it does not grant this app access to your Gmail or Google Calendar.

Notifications can show event details through Windows; choose the privacy setting to hide their content. The app must be running for desktop reminders. Windows, browsers and cloud providers can retain their own operational/history information. Calendar databases, automatic snapshots and exported backups are not encrypted by C.C. Lime. Windows encrypts a remembered sign-in token. Protect your device, exports and recovery copies appropriately.

The app implements no advertising or automatic product analytics. This does not describe all provider processing. Provider terms, subprocessors, support/log locations, retention and international transfers must be confirmed and linked by the operator before the final notice is issued. The observed `northamerica-northeast2` database region is not a promise that every service or retained copy stays in Canada.

You can edit your calendar/profile, export calendar or full backup data, sign out, remove the current local account data or delete your cloud account. These actions have different effects: sign-out leaves local calendar files; local removal leaves cloud data; account deletion removes managed active account records and identity through its bounded protocol. Other computers, exports, old recovery copies, browser/Windows history and provider-required retention can remain. A minimal deletion marker prevents stale clients from recreating deleted data. The operator must publish the approved purpose-based retention schedule and explain exceptions rather than promise immediate erasure everywhere.

Contact the named privacy contact for access, corrections, a retention/deletion request or a complaint. The operator will verify identity proportionately, explain the scope and any lawful exceptions, and respond under the applicable rules. You will never be asked to disclose your password or MFA/recovery secret. Complaints can also be raised with the applicable privacy authority. This draft does not itself create a functioning contact channel or demonstrate a handled request.

## Intake, identity and completion procedure

1. Receive the request through the approved channel; assign a private case ID and record receipt time, request type/scope, responsible officer and applicable due date. Acknowledge scope and accessible delivery needs. Use a known signed-in account/recent authentication or another proportionate approved check; ask for only what is needed. Escalate account-loss disputes to the privacy officer. Do not collect government identification routinely for this student product or ask for a password.
2. Where PIPEDA access rules apply, track the normal 30-day response period. Use an extension only on a lawful ground, with required notice within the original period; include the reason, new date and complaint rights. Have the named officer verify any exception. The [OPC access guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/accessing-personal-information/obligations-for-organizations/02_05_d_54_ati_02/) supplies the actual rule; a support estimate cannot override it.
3. Reconcile the request against [every holding in the inventory](PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md): identity/provider metadata, active local/cloud records, outbox/shadows/conflicts/history, receipts/tombstones/marker, automatic snapshots/quarantine, upgrade copies, user-selected exports, device/notification/browser history, administrator/support logs and vendor-held records. Identify what the operator controls, what requires vendor cooperation and what is held only by the requester/another computer. Record unknowns and retained exceptions explicitly.
4. Access: produce a readable account-specific copy and explain purposes, use and recipients; isolate unrelated people/credentials/internal security material. Verify every relevant store is represented, or explain its lawful exclusion. The app's backup/diagnostics alone are not a complete privacy-access response. Deliver through an approved authenticated protected channel and verify recipient access.
5. Correction: validate the requested change; use normal account/UI/provider operations for active state. Record a dispute where correction cannot be agreed. Reconcile replicas and any required recipient notification. Preserve legitimate audit/legal records with a justified restriction instead of silently rewriting evidence.
6. Deletion/withdrawal: distinguish product account deletion from broader retention rights. Obtain explicit scope confirmation and recent account authentication. Exercise resumable deletion; independently verify active identity and managed records/receipts/head removal, while preserving the anti-resurrection marker under the approved policy. Address identified retained copies/vendors/offline devices according to purpose, feasibility and legal holds. Do not purge an owner's backup or invent forensic SSD/provider erasure.
7. Complaint: record the concern, impartial assigned reviewer, evidence, findings and remedy; explain the available regulator path. Escalate suspected exposure through [incident procedures](INCIDENT_CONTINUITY.md). Do not send external notices without the named person's authorization.
8. Close only after the officer checks completeness, exceptions, secure delivery, timeliness and corrective actions. Record outcome and review date in restricted evidence. Apply the approved retention schedule to the request log and identity-check material; do not commit personal request contents to GitHub.

## Breach and retained-copy decision packet

When PIPEDA applies, assess sensitivity and likelihood of misuse to decide whether a breach presents a real risk of significant harm; record the rationale and obtain the accountable notification decision. Required regulator/individual notification and relevant contractual escalation must follow the actual facts and law, not a blanket “no patient data” exemption. Keep all breach records for at least 24 months after the organization determines the breach, with sufficient assessment detail, as described by [OPC breach guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/privacy-breaches-at-your-business/gd_pb_201810/). Counsel/officer must reconcile any longer applicable hold and the detailed notice contents. This is a proposal; no actual breach or notification is asserted.

## Synthetic end-to-end exercise after appointment

Use an owned disposable verified account, generated calendar/profile/history, a known automatic snapshot and a deliberately retained export. Capture each store's synthetic digest/count; introduce a correction; request access through the real appointed channel; exercise deletion; verify live active-store/identity removal independently; record the export/snapshot/marker/provider exceptions and their approved disposal or retention. Test account-loss identity verification and complaint/extension scenarios without sending real regulator communications. Measure receipt/acknowledgment/decision/response times and verify a second person can execute the procedure. Existing technical account-deletion/export tests are useful preparation, but cannot substitute for this operator/vendor/request-channel exercise.
