## Method and accountability

Proposed ordinal scale: likelihood 1 rare to 5 likely/frequent; impact 1 minor inconvenience to 5 major disclosure, unrecoverable loss or service compromise. Multiply to prioritize: 15–25 urgent, 8–14 high, 4–7 medium, 1–3 low. Scores assess remaining risk with current controls. These are engineering judgments, not measured probabilities or an ISO-prescribed method. Operator approval of method/treatment is pending. Advisory severity is separate.

Engineering identifies a workstream, not an appointed organizational officer. Proposed operator/privacy/security roles are unassigned. Due gates below are release dependencies, not approved calendar deadlines. Closure requires named accountable owner, evidence reference/hash, test date, reviewer, residual impact, signed decision and next review. Risk acceptance cannot supply missing certification or test evidence.

## Draft operating controls

Preview CI generates dependency evidence on pushes/PRs. Production dependency approval requires a fresh `npm run check:release-security`; it currently fails on **any** reported finding. Build success is insufficient. Evidence includes lockfile/artifact hashes. CI dependency artifacts retain 14 days, a tooling setting **not an approved audit-evidence retention policy**.

Proposed vulnerability triage targets for operator review: critical/high within one business day of discovery, other findings within five business days; record containment, owner, due date and recheck. These are not active monitoring or promised service levels. No scheduled monitoring service is created here. Review native Electron/Chromium separately; npm inventory is not their complete SBOM.

Review triggers: audience beyond owner, healthcare/customer contracts, region/vendor/data/retention changes, incident/advisory, release-channel or credential/admin changes. Establish an approved cadence and sustained independent-assessment evidence program. This register is not a Statement of Applicability or full normative mapping.
